Timeless finishes

Timeless beauty.
Naturally considered.

More than a beautiful wall. A thoughtful choice for your space.

Discover the beauty of lime-based Venetian plaster: a breathable mineral finish with natural character and lasting appeal. Depending on the product system and finish, lime-based plaster may allow water vapour to pass through and its alkalinity can help resist surface mould growth. Expertly applied and properly cared for, it creates a durable finish that develops character over time.
