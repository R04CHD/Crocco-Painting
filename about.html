About Crocco Painting & Venetian

Quality finishes.
Lasting impressions.

Beautiful spaces begin with thoughtful craftsmanship.

With 10 years of industry experience, Crocco Painting & Venetian brings together professional painting and bespoke decorative finishes for homes and commercial spaces across South East Melbourne.

From a fresh exterior to the subtle depth of Venetian plaster, we approach every project with care, precision and an understanding of your vision.

Your vision

Your space should reflect your style and the way you live or work. We take the time to understand your ideas, guide your choice of colours and finishes, and recommend an approach suited to your project.

Our purpose is simple: to create beautifully finished spaces through skilled application, carefully selected materials and personal service.

Why choose us?

Craftsmanship in every detail

From preparation to the final coat, we give each surface the attention it deserves.

Painting and decorative expertise

Interior and exterior painting, complemented by handcrafted Venetian plaster finishes that bring texture, depth and individuality to your space.

Materials chosen with purpose

We select quality paints and decorative coatings to suit the surface, the setting and your desired finish.

Respect for your space

Careful protection, a tidy work area and regular clean-ups help keep your home or business cared for throughout the project.

Our credentials and cover include:

  • Construction Induction Training — White Card
  • Yellow Card — Vertical, Scissor & Boom Lifts
  • Working at Heights training
  • Public Liability Insurance
