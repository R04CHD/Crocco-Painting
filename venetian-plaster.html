Bring a distinctive sense of warmth and sophistication to your interiors with Venetian plaster and bespoke decorative finishes.

We create handcrafted surfaces that respond beautifully to light, adding depth, movement and individuality to residential and commercial spaces across Melbourne.

From soft, understated textures to expressive stone-inspired finishes, each surface is shaped by the application process.

A finish that feels uniquely yours.

Your walls can become a defining part of your interior. We work with you to explore colours, textures and levels of sheen that complement your architecture, furnishings and overall vision.

Whether you’re drawn to a subtle mineral finish or a statement wall with pronounced relief, we help you find a direction that feels right for your space.

  • Reception areas and boutique retail interiors

From inspiration to application.

We begin with your ideas and assess the surfaces you’d like to transform. Together, we consider the texture, colour and intended use before selecting a suitable finish.
